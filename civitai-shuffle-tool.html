🎲 Civitai Shuffle

Collect images with the Civitai Post Collector extension and export them as a list, or save a Civitai page as HTML — either way, drop the file here and this shows them all in random order. If the export is a list of post IDs (from a model page), each post is unpacked into all of its images. Everything happens in your browser; nothing you upload goes anywhere else.

2

No extension? On civitai.com or civitai.red, scroll to load images, then Ctrl/Cmd+S → "Webpage, HTML Only" instead. You can also paste post IDs or /posts/… links.
